Import a utility's plans

Drop the plan the way the utility published it: an Excel project list, a CSV, or GIS files (KML/KMZ, shapefile as a .zip or the .shp, .dbf and .prj together, GeoJSON). Column names are matched loosely, so owner, voltage, isd and latitude all work, and title rows above a spreadsheet's header are skipped. Tables need coordinates for each project; GIS files carry their own. Year-only dates like 2029 are fine.

    Unit costs
    
    
    Planning estimates, not quotes. Each shared item's saving is worked out from these numbers, and every pair updates when one changes. When one utility's cost is shared, each side saves its share (50% by default); a coordinated outage and a crossing designed once count in full. Check them against USDA NASS land values, the MISO Transmission Cost Estimation Guide, and local crane and heavy-haul rates.
