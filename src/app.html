Construction timeline bars run from estimated start to planned in-service; flagged projects only

Data. DESC: SCRTP 2026 to 2030 project descriptions ($2M and above). Georgia: SERTP 2026 preliminary expansion plan and 2025 SERTP report. Georgia's grid is planned jointly by Georgia Power, Georgia Transmission and MEAG, so all three appear as one system.
Assumptions. Plans name substations, not coordinates, so locations are placed by hand at the named town or plant; dashed lines mark best guesses. Plans list in-service dates only (the Georgia regional plan gives the year), so construction start is estimated from project type, voltage and length. Georgia Power's own project pages for Callaway Road to Thomson and Effingham County publish start milestones but no in-service date, so those end dates are estimates. Grey diamonds and lines are existing assets for reference, including the Thomson to Vogtle 500 kV line (in service 2018) and DESC's Stevens Creek hydro plant in Martinez, Georgia.
Cost model. Rough and planning-level. Uses the plan's cost where listed (all DESC projects); otherwise unit costs of about $2.5M/km for new 500 kV line, $1.4M/km for 230 kV, $0.95M/km for 115 kV, half that for rebuilds, and $60M, $25M or $8M for 500, 230 or 115 kV substations. Savings: crews and equipment, half of a 5% mobilization cost on the smaller project (same build window, under 40 km); one shared laydown yard, $400K (under 8 km); shared right-of-way, 1 km of 45 m corridor at $37K/ha plus access road and permits (under 1.6 km); one coordinated outage, $250K (touching).
